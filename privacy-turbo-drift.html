Turbo Drift – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Turbo Drift (“the app”) is a free street-racing game. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information on our own servers. Your coins, upgrades and best distance are saved only on your device and are never sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address and other device information to show ads and measure their performance. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device’s Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

Contact
